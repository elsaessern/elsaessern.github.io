Since 2022, I have received training in the Engaged Learning, Inclusive Teaching, and Equity Professional Development program. This is a joint effort from researchers (funded by the [NSF](https://www.nsf.gov/awardsearch/showAward?AWD_ID=2013563)) at Oregon State University, Virginia Commenwealth University, and San Diego State University to address concerns in the training that graduate students in mathematics receive. In particular, graduate students typically receive a large amount of training as researchers compared to very little training as educators. However, the majority of these students end up in teaching-heavy roles. Moreover, undergraduate mathematics education is commonly not engaging, inclusive, and equitable. You can find additional info [here](https://aaas-iuse.org/resource/mathematics-graduate-teaching-assistant-professional-development-focused-on-implementation-of-eviden/).

Program steps and my involvement
------
The program is comprised of five main steps. 
1. First-Year Orientation
2. Graduate Teaching Seminar
3. Intro Course: Equity and Active Learning
4. Advanced Course: Equity and Active Learning
5. Peer Mentoring Experience

I am currently (February, 2025) on step 5. I completed the first four steps in years one and two of my graduate studies.
